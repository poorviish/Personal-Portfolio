Beyond the Resume

A little more about me

I'm studying Chemical Science & Technology at IIT Guwahati, but somewhere along the way I realized I enjoyed solving business problems more than the core chemistry ones.

Internships, case competitions, and student leadership gave me the chance to work on products, strategy, and operations, and I found myself drawn to problems that don't have obvious answers. I enjoy understanding why something isn't working, talking to users, and building solutions that actually make a difference.

Outside work, you'll usually find me organizing campus events, working on case competitions, or reading about products and startups. These days, I'm spending a lot of time exploring AI, product analytics, and how great product teams make decisions.
